What is BACON?

We're the largest STEM club at Charlottesville High School, where students participate in self-directed subgroups on activities of their own choosing. We have various subgroups in a wide range of topics, and students can start new ones if they would like! Currently we meet afterschool for an hour every week, and we have subgroups engaged in robotics, sewing, printed circuit board design, web development, game development, drone flying, Technology Student Association, and more!

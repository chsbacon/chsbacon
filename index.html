What is CHS BACON?

BACON (Best All Around Club of Nerds)BACON (Best All Around Club of Nerds) is the internationally acclaimed science club at Charlottesville High School. Members join various subgroups in topics like robotics (FTC), environmental advocacy (Green Bacon), electrionics design (Hack Club), and sewing!

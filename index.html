What is CHS BACON?

BACON (Best All Around Club of Nerds)BACON (Best All Around Club of Nerds) is the internationally acclaimed science club at Charlottesville High School. Established in 2010, the club has represented Virginia in elite competitions such as the MIT- and NASA-sponsored Zero Robotics programming tournament. In addition to competitions, students work in self-directed teams in areas such as environmental impact (Green BACON), robotics (FTC), and more!
